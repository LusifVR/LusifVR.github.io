01 // Who We Are

Welcome To UHR

The United Hollow Republic (UHR) is a virtual-reality military simulation community that hosts organized events and operations. Through a player-guided story, members take part in an ongoing campaign featuring simulated combat scenarios, combined arms, logistics, and a focus on realism and unit cohesion.

Our goal is to provide an immersive experience with scenarios that are constantly evolving. We believe the best experiences come from the people who take part in them, and our community plays an active role in shaping what comes next.

Our Community

UHR is about more than scheduled operations. Members can take part in weekly training, movie nights, competitions, game nights, and plenty of other community activities.
