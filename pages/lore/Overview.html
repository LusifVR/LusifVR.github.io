OVERVIEW

SETTING

ARES takes place in an alternate reality. The earth we live on, the land it formed, the countries it inherited, and societies established differ from ours. Taking place in real-time, 30 years in the future, the geopolitical situation continues to escalate, making warfare no longer black and white. Former alliances, neighbors, and century-long treaties all begin to crumble under the weight of the world's struggle to maintain stability.

SUMMARY

For decades, the world has been shaped by research known as the Hannibal Project, an abandoned space program dating back to the early 1980s. Humanity was ambitious and looking to the stars. However, disagreements and a heated geopolitical situation halted nearly all of the progress. At the heart of Hannibal was Terbium, a rare earth material capable of producing enormous amounts of magnetic and kinetic energy. What was originally intended to fuel humanity's exploration of the stars became lost to generations. That was until the first use of weaponized Terbium. On April 9th, 1993, one of the world's leading powers, the International Trade Federation (ITF), developed and tested a WMD on the island nation of Eto. This WMD would later be confirmed to be a converted Hannibal rocket. Eto had lost 81% of its population. The world opened its eyes in horror as realization set in: who controlled Terbium controlled history. The decades that followed became defined by conflict. The ITF expanded throughout the region, using its control of resources and military technology to establish its influence. Nations formed alliances, proxy wars erupted, and an escalating arms race spread across the Paraq and Atuex regions. Naming themselves after the slang for regional pirate origin, known as 'Hollows', in 2018, three northern nations united to form the United Hollow Republic (UHR), created to protect its members from the growing threat of the ITF and the conflicts spreading throughout the region. What began as a defensive alliance eventually became a major military power. The UHR would enter a cold war against the ITF for several decades to follow, as it focused on returning any stability it could to the Atuex region. Nearly 20 years later, in 2041, the tension between the two nations would boil. The ITF would declare war on the UHR as it began pressing east into the Paraq Ocean, towards Republic waters. This would mark the start of The Paraq War. The ITF's reach would push east, deep into other nations, taking control and establishing puppet states in its wake. It would take the combined effort of the Republic and several other participating nations over a decade to finally bring the ITF's momentum to a halt. In 2052, when the UHR landed on the shores of Shangashi, the ITF's capital city, it would mark the downfall of the Federation and the end of the ITF. By 2056, the situation has spiraled far beyond a simple war between nations. False-flag operations, forged enemy communications, covert alliances, stolen research, and deliberately manipulated international relations have turned the world into a web of conflicting interests. And beneath the wars is an even bigger problem: the world's critical resources are running out. The world scrambles to keep itself together as age-old alliances turn on each other.
